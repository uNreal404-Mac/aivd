## Toonflow

The node canvas at `/creative-space/canvas` (`src/components/creative-space/canvas/`,
`src/lib/creative-space/canvas-graph.ts`) follows the interaction design of
Toonflow's canvas: typed ports and connection checks, structure-only undo,
dragging a wire into empty space to add a compatible node, group frames,
layered auto-arrange and node toolbars. It is our own React implementation;
no Toonflow source file, asset, name or service is used, and all generation
goes through this site's own API and credits.

Source: https://github.com/HBAI-Ltd/Toonflow-app
